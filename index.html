AI Regulation and Data Center Siting

We predicted which US counties could host a data center, first from infrastructure, then with state AI and data center legislation added. This page shows where the model expects data centers, where policy changes that expectation, and how both compare with a national lab forecast model which doesn't use policy data.

Read this with care
  • These are associations. Nothing here shows that a law caused a siting decision.
  • Policy features are set by state, so every county in a state shares them and policy changes follow state lines.
  • Existing data centers come from OpenStreetMap and include facilities of every size.
  • PNNL modeled 37 states. Hatched counties have no PNNL forecast or no prediction from our model, which is different from a forecast of none.
  • Probabilities are out-of-fold: every county is scored by models trained without its state.
